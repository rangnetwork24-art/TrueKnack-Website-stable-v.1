Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TrueKnack courses, fees, eligibility, placements, and centers.

Course & Program Details

TrueKnack offers three primary placement-focused retail banking programs:
  • Smart Banker Program: A 3-month job-ready online certificate in retail banking & financial services.
  • Aurix Bankers Program: A 3-month job-assured program in collaboration with AU Small Finance Bank.
  • Equitas Ignite Program: An intensive 2-week job-assured initiative with Equitas Small Finance Bank to launch your career as a Business Development Officer.
The Smart Banker Program is delivered online over 3 months. The Aurix Bankers Program is an intensive 1-month program in partnership with AU Small Finance Bank. The Equitas Ignite Program is a 2-week focused initiative.
Upon completing the Smart Banker Program, you receive a Certificate in Banking & Financial Services. For the Aurix Bankers Program, you are awarded a professional Banking Operations Certification along with your confirmed placement as an Assistant Manager with AU Small Finance Bank.

Fees & Loan Options

  • Smart Banker Program: ₹ 50,000 + GST (Flexible installment options available).
  • Aurix Bankers Program: Please contact our admissions advisor for details on fee structures, sponsorship, and scholarship options.
Yes. Both programs support flexible installment plans, and the Smart Banker Program offers 0% interest education loan options.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility criteria generally require:
  • Smart Banker Program: Graduates or postgraduates aged 21-28 years with min. 50% marks in 10th, 12th & Graduation.
  • Aurix Bankers Program: Regular graduates aged 21-28 years with min. 50% marks.
No prior finance background is required. The programs are designed to train graduates from any stream and prepare them completely for professional banking operations.

Placement Support

TrueKnack provides 100% placement support. We have successfully placed over 6,000 graduates across leading private sector banks and retail financial institutions.
Graduates get placed in leading private banks, including Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IDFC First Bank, Bandhan Bank, and AU Small Finance Bank.

Training Locations

The Smart Banker Program is delivered online. The Aurix Bankers Program classroom training takes place at the residential campus of Baddi University, HP, with on-the-job training conducted at AU Small Finance Bank branches. Counseling and screenings are held at our verified Maharashtra centers (Thane, Pune, Mumbai, etc.).

Smart Banker Program

Smart Banker Program is a specialized program designed for fresh graduates and postgraduates to prepare them as 'Smart Bankers'. Candidates undergo an intensive 3-month training conducted by seasoned bankers with domain expertise, after which they are placed in leading private sector banks.

Till March 2026, TrueKnack has Trained & Placed more than 6,000 graduates in leading Indian private sector banks.

Modern Teaching Methods

PEDAGOGIES

Group Discussions

Students participate in interactive discussions to share ideas, enhance communication skills, and develop analytical thinking on banking and financial topics.

Branch Visit

Practical demonstrations through bank branch visits help students understand real-world banking operations and customer service processes.

Problem Solving

Students work on banking and financial case-based problems to strengthen decision-making and critical thinking abilities.

Gaming

Interactive learning through educational games helps students understand banking concepts, products, and customer handling scenarios in an engaging way.

Role Play

Master banking concepts through real-life roleplay scenarios involving customer interactions, sales conversations, account opening, and service activities.

Diagrams and Graphs

Visual learning through charts, diagrams, and financial reports helps students understand complex banking concepts more effectively.

Case Study Analysis

Students analyze real banking and financial industry case studies to understand practical challenges, compliance requirements, and business solutions.

Mock Interviews & Personality Development

Regular mock interviews, aptitude sessions, and personality development activities prepare students for banking recruitment processes and corporate careers.
